The baseline prompt, You write poems about the topic you are given., never passed: its twelve poems ran 92 to 205 words. The first rewrite overshot the other way (27 to 41 words, two of twelve passing). The second put ten of twelve in range, and the fourth eleven of twelve, at 40 to 65 words.

Each rewrite is one call to the reflection agent, which reads the held candidate's twelve transcripts and their scores and appends rules to the prompt, such as WHEN writing any poem, DO aim for 40-60 words total. All observed poems were significantly too long (92-205 words). That costs 180,000 to 310,000 tokens per rewrite, against about a thousand per poem: the search, not the agent, is the expensive part.

In the panel, the Progress tab shows each candidate's pass rate with its 95% interval; open candidate 4 for its prompt, shown as a diff against the candidate it was rewritten from, and any trial for the poem itself.
